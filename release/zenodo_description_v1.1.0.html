A hand-curated set of 17 protein regions across 8 proteins, shown experimentally to be required for, or to constitute the core of, protein aggregation. Every row was established by opening the source paper and checking the claim against it.

The dataset carries four columns that aggregation datasets usually lack, each because omitting it produces a benchmark that silently measures the wrong thing:

  • construct — which construct established the region. A region can be necessary in one construct and dispensable in another, and both results can be correct.
  • circularity_status — whether a predictor was in the discovery path. A region found by a predictor cannot be used to test that predictor. Circular rows are kept and labelled, not dropped.
  • stratum — whether experiment and prediction agree. Regions that are mapped cleanly tend to be the ones predictors find, so a dataset of well-mapped regions is enriched for agreement; only discordant rows can discriminate between predictors.
  • cpad_status — new in 1.1.0. Whether the row's protein is in CPAD 2.0, and therefore whether a modern predictor's hit on it may be recall of training data rather than prediction. This is a third kind of circularity, distinct from the discovery path and from experiment–prediction agreement.

The contamination check is the main change in 1.1.0, and its result is sobering. All 17 rows were checked against all three CPAD 2.0 datasets, each read complete, plus an orthologue check in both OMA and OrthoInspector, because the CPAD90 training set transfers annotations to homologues. Six of the eight proteins are CPAD members. Of the five rows in the discordant stratum — the only stratum that can discriminate between predictors — three are CPAD-contaminated and two are not. A reader benchmarking a modern predictor on this dataset without that column would measure memorisation on most of the rows that appear to matter.

Limitations are stated in the data descriptor and should be read before use. The dataset is small and is not a random sample. After excluding CPAD-contaminated rows, the usable discordant stratum holds 2 rows, which supports a case series rather than a rate. 8 of 17 rows are not yet assessed for stratum. Rows are not independent. Most rows are recombinant or cell-based and cannot speak to aggregation in patients. The CPAD90 membership list is unpublished, so no row can be certified training-set-clean in absolute terms.
